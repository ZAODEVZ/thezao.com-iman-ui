Governance

Earned, not bought

In most communities a vote is something you buy: hold the most tokens, hold the most power. The ZAO takes the opposite position. Here, a vote is something you earn, through Respect: an onchain reputation built by showing up and contributing, ranked by peers each week. It cannot be bought, sold, or transferred.

The whitepaper lays out the theory, the mechanics, and the verifiable on-chain architecture in full.
